Tap a charge to detonate it. Its fuse is also its blast reach — a 5 carries five waves through touching charges, a 1 only kills itself. Score is the fuses caught × waves², so depth beats value: tap the tip of a cluster, never the middle. Every turn all fuses drop by one. A charge that hits 0 is rubble, permanently. You lose when there's no room to drop the next batch.
